# Engineering Gates — the live-enforcement registry

> **What this is:** the single honest map from every engineering quality domain (and each of the 12 architecture principles) to its LIVE enforcement: the CI job, lint rule, drift-guard test, or mandatory review checklist that makes it real. Where a claim maps to nothing, the row says **UNENFORCED**, with a disposition. The purpose: nothing in our standards is "just documentation" — it is either enforced, or its gap is visible and owned.
> **Scope:** this registry maps the **`apps/web`** app (the live site). The `apps/sandbox` work-front (uncommitted WIP) has its own eslint/vitest/tsconfig and is governed by `docs/sandbox-app/SANDBOX_RULES.md` + its gated per-slice battery — it is deliberately NOT yet wired into the web CI page-lists (pa11y/Lighthouse) or `--filter`ed into `ci.yml`; full CI wiring is a promotion-to-public gate in `docs/sandbox-app/BUILD_ORDER.md`.
> **Scope correction (2026-08-29):** the "uncommitted WIP" framing above is stale. `apps/sandbox` is **LIVE + PUBLIC at `app.diboas.com` since 2026-08-22** (still `noindex`, still play money, geofence armed), committed on `main`, and gated by its own `pnpm --filter sandbox screen-check` battery — root `type-check`, all four suites, build, knip, and the repo-wide `format:check` CI runs. What remains true is the web-CI gap: the pa11y/Lighthouse page-lists and the `ci.yml` `--filter`s still do not cover it — tracked as PENDING_ALL **5.122**. **Updated 2026-09-12 (Handoff §3.1-§3.4):** `main` is now **protected** with required checks `quality` · `Secrets scan (gitleaks)` · `Dependency vulnerability audit`, force-pushes and deletions blocked, and `deleteBranchOnMerge = true` (the setting whose absence caused #589 and #591 to merge into their own bases). `strict` and `enforce_admins` are deliberately `false`; `lighthouse`/`e2e`/`pa11y` are deliberately not required yet, because the `5.88` font-fetch flake already needs a build retry and a flaky required check blocks every merge. `screen-check`'s build leg is now `pnpm -w build` (`5.293`) — it had been sandbox-only, the third instance of one defect in that file.
> **Sibling systems:** the content gates live in `docs/tech/ux-governance/` (CI-guarded by `validate:ux-canon`); process playbooks in `docs/playbooks/` (local); the recurring/scheduled jobs (crons + timed workflows) are cataloged in `docs/tech/infrastructure.md` §8 → _Scheduled jobs — every cadence in one place_. This file is git-tracked and audited whenever CI composition changes.

**Gate-class rule for the weekly `/market` workflow (2026-08-31, PENDING_ALL 5.151):** gates split by _who owns the field they assert on_. **Bot-written output** (`generate.mjs --check`, jargon, market + analytics-sdk vitest, prettier) blocks **before** the PR opens — the bot can satisfy these by regenerating, so blocking keeps bad data out of a PR. **Human-owned fields** (the research-memo voice, which `generate.mjs` explicitly never writes) are **reported** in the workflow and enforced on the **PR's own CI**. Placing a human-owned invariant in a bot pre-flight is a deadlock: it cannot be satisfied by the actor running it, and on 2026-08-31 it blocked PR creation entirely, defeating the ratified AUTO-HOLD policy. **A gate must be enforced where the actor who can satisfy it is standing.** No honesty guarantee is weakened by the split — `memoFigureReconciliation` still blocks the merge.
